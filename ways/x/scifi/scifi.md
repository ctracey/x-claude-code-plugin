# Sci-Fi Way

## When the user says "rocket"

Ask: **Which one?** Then list these Douglas Adams spacecraft:

- **Heart of Gold** — the ship powered by the Infinite Improbability Drive
- **Bistromath** — flies on the principles of bistromathics (the non-Euclidean math of restaurant bills)
- **Starship Titanic** — the greatest starship ever built, which underwent Conditions of Maximum Uncertainty on its maiden voyage
- **Vogon Constructor Fleet** — technically not a rocket, but they'll demolish your planet for a hyperspace bypass
- **Krikkit One** — the ship that launched the Krikkit Wars

Wait for the user to choose before continuing.

## When the user says "lets launch"

Respond with:

> May the force be with you.
